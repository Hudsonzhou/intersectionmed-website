Support around your appointment

01

At the hospital

Request interpretation for registration, consultation communication, provider instructions and navigation. Confirm the campus, appointment time, duration and any waiting-time arrangements.

02

Written documents

Discuss which reports or letters need translation, their length, target language and turnaround. Certified translation, where required, needs separate confirmation with an appropriate provider.

03

After the appointment

Ask about report organization, translating questions and relaying the care team's response. Interpretation conveys clinical information; it does not provide an independent medical opinion.

Before you request coordination

  • Share the service or appointment, approximate dates and communication language.
  • Tell us whether an appointment is already booked and whether you need accompaniment, written translation or travel support.
  • Mention whether relevant records exist; do not send passport numbers, payment details or full medical files in the initial inquiry.

Ask about your support and fee

The coordination fee is discussed for your request. Confirm the support included, time on site and any separately charged hospital or travel services.

Discuss Your Visit

Questions before you travel

Can I book only an interpreter?

Yes, you can inquire about an appointment you have booked yourself. Share its location, date, language and support needs so availability and fees can be discussed.

How is the fee confirmed?

The request may involve time on site, travel, written documents and follow-up. Contact us with the scope and ask what is included, how waiting or overtime is handled and what is charged separately.
