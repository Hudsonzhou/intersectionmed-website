Practical questions for international visitors

Do you support an appointment I have already booked?

You can request English-Chinese interpretation or accompaniment separately. Share the institution, exact campus, appointment date and support window. Availability and fees need confirmation.

Is medical document translation the same as an interpreter?

No. Spoken interpretation supports a live conversation; written translation conveys a document. Tell us which you need, the language and expected timing. Certification, where required by an insurer or institution, must be confirmed separately.

How are waiting time and follow-up handled?

Discuss the agreed time on site, waiting or overtime, travel between campuses and any later document work before confirming. Clinical questions are relayed to the care team; interpretation does not replace medical advice.

Public and private options can be discussed. Institution requirements, appointment availability and medical charges must be confirmed for your request.
