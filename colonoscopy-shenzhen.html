Plan beyond the procedure time

Preparation and the return journey

The care team must explain preparation, sedation-related companion needs and when travel or other activities are appropriate. Do not plan an immediate flight or an unaccompanied return on an assumption.

Reports may follow later

Biopsy or pathology results can take longer than the appointment itself. Confirm report delivery, a clinician's explanation and further-care arrangements before leaving.

Before you request coordination

  • Share the service or appointment, approximate dates and communication language.
  • Tell us whether an appointment is already booked and whether you need accompaniment, written translation or travel support.
  • Mention whether relevant records exist; do not send passport numbers, payment details or full medical files in the initial inquiry.

Ask about your support and fee

The coordination fee is discussed for your request. Confirm the support included, time on site and any separately charged hospital or travel services.

Discuss Your Visit

Questions before you travel

Is a 2-3 day stay enough?

It can be an initial planning window for some visits, but consultation, preparation, procedure availability, reports and follow-up must be checked with the institution before travel.

Do you supply preparation or medication instructions?

No. Instructions and any changes to medication must come from the healthcare institution or licensed clinician. We assist with communication.
