Plan a digestive screening visit in Shenzhen

Coordinate the consultation, provider-issued preparation, appointment day and report follow-up.

Identify the appointment being discussed

Gastroscopy, colonoscopy and an initial gastroenterology consultation are different requests. A clinician determines which examination, if any, is appropriate and whether further assessment is needed.

Plan four stages rather than one appointment

StageConfirm with the provider
Clinical consultationWhether assessment or review of prior records is needed before scheduling.
PreparationHow and when provider instructions are delivered and questions answered.
Hospital attendanceRegistration, consent, language support and any companion requirements.
Results and follow-upReport timing, clinical explanation, further appointments and travel-related instructions.

Keep medical questions with the care team

Questions about medicines, food or drink, sedation, preparation, aftercare and return travel require provider instructions. IntersectionMed can help communicate those questions and translate the response.

Allow flexibility

A 2-3 day window can be a starting point for some planned visits, not a guarantee that every stage will be completed. Confirm the actual appointment and report timeline before fixing travel.

IntersectionMed is not a hospital or healthcare provider and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Medical decisions are made solely by licensed physicians and healthcare institutions.
