Prepare for a short dental visit in Shenzhen

Clarify a check, cleaning or simple filling with the dentist, and keep complex treatment outside a short-visit assumption.

Start with the assessment, not a treatment promise

Tell the provider whether you are asking about a check, cleaning or simple filling. Ask which earlier images or records it needs. The dentist confirms what is appropriate and whether it can be scheduled during your stay.

Ask for the stages in writing

StageQuestions
AssessmentWhich examination and imaging are proposed, and how are they charged?
Treatment discussionWhat options, materials, limitations and alternatives will the dentist explain?
SchedulingAre laboratory work, healing intervals or additional visits part of the proposed plan?
Follow-upWhat review or ongoing care is expected, and what happens after travelling home?

Compare the practical proposal

Ask for the appointment dates, cost breakdown, payment arrangements and the language of records. Confirm coordination and interpretation fees separately. A consultation does not guarantee a particular treatment or result.

Keep staged care outside the short-visit offer

Implants, orthodontics, complex root-canal treatment and laboratory-made restorations are not our core short-visit packages. If the dentist proposes further care, ask about all stages and follow-up before fixing travel. A short initial consultation does not make the whole course of treatment short.
