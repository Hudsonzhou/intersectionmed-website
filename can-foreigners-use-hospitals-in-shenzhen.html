Foreign-passport registration: questions before a hospital visit

Clarify registration documents, name matching and booking channels with the institution before arrival.

Confirm the registration route

Ask the institution which booking channel accepts your identity document, whether an account is needed and whether registration is completed online or on arrival. Requirements vary by institution and department.

Keep identity details consistent

  • Use the name format requested by the institution, matching your travel document.
  • Ask which original identity document must be shown at registration.
  • Confirm the contact number or email used for appointment messages.
  • If booking for someone else, ask about any authorization requirements.

Prepare without oversharing

For an initial inquiry to IntersectionMed, a general service request, dates and preferred language are enough to begin. Do not enter a passport number or attach sensitive records. When an institution requires documents, confirm a suitable sharing method first.

If an online system does not work

Record the booking channel, the step that failed and any non-sensitive error message. Contact the institution's official service channel or ask about coordination support. Avoid making a second booking or payment without checking whether the first was recorded.
