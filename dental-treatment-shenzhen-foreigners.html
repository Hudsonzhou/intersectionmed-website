Dental follow-up after returning home

Discuss records, provider contact and further appointments before leaving Shenzhen.

Ask about ongoing care before agreeing to a plan

Ask the dental provider which follow-up appointments are expected, whether further visits in Shenzhen are part of the plan and which questions require in-person assessment.

Request the relevant records

  • Ask for the provider's written plan and any documents it can release.
  • Confirm access to available images and reports.
  • Keep payment records and provider-issued information about materials or procedures where applicable.
  • Ask which information a clinician at home may need and in what language.

Confirm the follow-up contact

Find out how to reach the dental team with clinical questions and what information it needs to identify the visit. Translation support may help communication but does not replace assessment by a licensed professional.

Discuss additional costs and travel

Ask whether further consultations, translation, document work or return travel are separate charges. If arrangements change, confirm the provider's and coordination service's change terms individually.
