Questions before a colonoscopy-related visit

A conversation checklist for the clinician and institution, covering scheduling and communication.

Ask about the initial assessment

  • Is a gastroenterology consultation needed before scheduling?
  • Which previous reports or procedure records should the clinician review?
  • How should medication and allergy information be provided securely?

Ask how instructions will reach you

Confirm the language and contact channel for provider-issued preparation, consent and aftercare information. Ask whom to contact if an instruction is unclear or your circumstances change.

Ask about attendance arrangements

  • What arrival and registration time should you plan for?
  • Are there companion or discharge arrangements to confirm?
  • What questions should be answered before arranging return travel?
  • When will the clinician explain findings and any further documents?

Use the checklist to support a conversation

These questions do not recommend colonoscopy or provide preparation instructions. The licensed clinician and institution must assess the request and supply the medical guidance.
