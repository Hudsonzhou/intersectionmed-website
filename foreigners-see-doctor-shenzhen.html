Patient Guides / Hospital access

The day at the hospital: registration to report collection

Understand the practical handoffs during a planned Shenzhen appointment.

Find the right building and registration desk

Use the exact campus and department from your confirmation. Ask whether an original identity document, booking reference or registration payment is needed before joining the consultation queue.

Make the consultation easier to follow

Prepare a brief list of questions and tell the care team which language you understand. If interpretation is arranged, speak in short sections and ask for clarification before agreeing to the next step.

Confirm any additional steps

A clinician may discuss tests, other departments or another visit. Ask the provider where to go, whether another booking is needed, how charges are confirmed and when results will be ready. Clinical recommendations come from the care team.

Before leaving the building

  • Keep receipts and provider-issued documents.
  • Confirm report collection or online access instructions.
  • Ask who will explain results and how to arrange follow-up.
  • Confirm how to contact the institution with later questions.
