Support around your appointment

01

Clarify the requested appointment

Endoscopy is a broad term. Tell us whether your request concerns gastroscopy (upper gastrointestinal endoscopy), colonoscopy or a consultation to discuss the next step. The clinician confirms the procedure.

02

Understand the provider's requirements

Confirm appointment timing, provider-issued preparation instructions, consent and any companion arrangements. Request language support before arrival.

03

Keep reports and follow-up connected

Ask when documents will be available and how to obtain a clinician's explanation or further consultation. Translation and document organization can be discussed separately.

Plan beyond the procedure time

Preparation and the return journey

The care team must explain preparation, sedation-related companion needs and when travel or other activities are appropriate. Do not plan an immediate flight or an unaccompanied return on an assumption.

Reports may follow later

Biopsy or pathology results can take longer than the appointment itself. Confirm report delivery, a clinician's explanation and further-care arrangements before leaving.

General background: NHS endoscopy information

Before you request coordination

  • Share the service or appointment, approximate dates and communication language.
  • Tell us whether an appointment is already booked and whether you need accompaniment, written translation or travel support.
  • Mention whether relevant records exist; do not send passport numbers, payment details or full medical files in the initial inquiry.

Ask about your support and fee

The coordination fee is discussed for your request. Confirm the support included, time on site and any separately charged hospital or travel services.

Discuss Your Visit

Questions before you travel

Can I ask for gastroscopy and colonoscopy together?

You can raise the request with the institution. Whether both are appropriate and how they are scheduled must be decided by the clinician.

What should I send initially?

Share the appointment you are seeking, approximate dates and language needs. Describe whether previous reports exist; wait to agree an appropriate channel before sharing records.
