Practical questions for international visitors

Can I request a check and cleaning rather than a full treatment plan?

Yes. Describe that request and your dates. The dentist confirms the assessment, whether cleaning or simple care is appropriate, its written costs and whether it can take place during your stay.

What should be confirmed before an appointment?

Confirm the institution and campus, registration documents, assessment charges, interpretation time and how any additional care would be approved. Do not send images or full records in an initial inquiry.

What if the dentist proposes more complex care?

Ask for all stages, laboratory or healing intervals, follow-up and costs before deciding. Implants, orthodontics and complex restorative work are not advertised as our one- or two-appointment packages.

Public and private options can be discussed. Institution requirements, appointment availability and medical charges must be confirmed for your request.

Before you request coordination

  • Share the service or appointment, approximate dates and communication language.
  • Tell us whether an appointment is already booked and whether you need accompaniment, written translation or travel support.
  • Mention whether relevant records exist; do not send passport numbers, payment details or full medical files in the initial inquiry.

Ask about your support and fee

The coordination fee is discussed for your request. Confirm the support included, time on site and any separately charged hospital or travel services.

Discuss Your Visit

Questions before you travel

Can a check and cleaning fit one visit?

This may be possible after the dental provider confirms the appointment and your needs. Additional findings can change the scope, timing and follow-up.

What if I need more than basic care?

Ask the dentist for a staged proposal and follow-up plan before deciding. Do not assume the full treatment can be finished during the current trip.
