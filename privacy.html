Privacy Notice

How we handle information submitted through the IntersectionMed website.

Information You Share

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, country or region, preferred travel dates, language needs, and a general description of the support you are seeking. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and consider practical coordination options for a planned healthcare visit.

Please Do Not Send Sensitive Medical Records Through the Website Form

Do not submit passport numbers, payment information, detailed medical records, imaging files, or other highly sensitive personal information through the website form or ordinary email. If additional documents are needed after an initial response, we will discuss an appropriate way to share them.

How Website Enquiries Are Delivered

The website enquiry form uses FormSubmit to deliver your message to IntersectionMed at intersectionmed@gmail.com. Submitting the form sends the information you enter through that service. We recommend sharing only the minimum information needed for an initial, non-emergency inquiry.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your request for coordination support.
  • To communicate about appointment, translation, travel, and practical planning questions.
  • To improve the clarity and operation of our services.

Our Role

IntersectionMed is a healthcare coordination and patient support platform. We do not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Medical decisions are made solely by licensed physicians and healthcare institutions.

Questions About Privacy

For privacy questions, contact intersectionmed@gmail.com.

This notice is written to explain the website workflow clearly. It is not a substitute for legal advice. Update it when your data-handling process, contact channels, or legal requirements change.
