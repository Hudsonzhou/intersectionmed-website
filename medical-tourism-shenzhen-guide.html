Your Shenzhen stay: accommodation, transport and daily essentials

A travel checklist around a confirmed healthcare appointment, with room for schedule changes.

Choose accommodation after the appointment location

Check the actual route to the confirmed campus, accessibility, room arrangements for a companion and the hotel's guest-registration requirements. A familiar district name alone does not establish a short hospital journey.

Keep essential information available

  • Institution and hotel names and addresses in Chinese and English.
  • Appointment confirmation and official contact channels.
  • A working phone connection and a way to contact your coordinator if arranged.
  • Payment methods confirmed with the hospital and accommodation provider.
  • Transport options for arrival and the appointment day.

Build flexibility into the itinerary

A consultation, procedure, report and follow-up may fall on different dates. Check the provider's schedule and instructions before planning sightseeing, onward travel or a fixed return journey.

Use current travel sources

Check Shenzhen Airport for current arrival information and official Mainland immigration information for entry questions. A hospital appointment does not by itself establish permission to enter.
