Questions worth asking before you travel

How are coordination fees calculated?

Support depends on the appointment, language needs, time on site and any follow-up assistance. Contact us for a discussion of your request and the applicable fee. Hospital and travel costs should also be confirmed separately.

Can you help with public and private hospitals?

We can help coordinate options in both settings. The department, language support, appointment availability and provider requirements need to be checked for each request.

Do I need to book flights before contacting you?

You can inquire before choosing travel dates. Check appointment timing, any provider requirements and travel documents before making fixed arrangements.

IntersectionMed is not a hospital or healthcare provider and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Medical decisions are made solely by licensed physicians and healthcare institutions.
