Practical questions for international visitors

Can foreigners arrange a checkup without a local booking app?

Ask the institution which channel accepts a foreign passport, how the name should be entered and which original document to show. We can help communicate registration requirements; an inquiry is not a confirmed booking.

What should I compare before choosing a package?

Request the included consultations and tests, report language, expected delivery dates and any separately charged items. Women's and men's health requests can be discussed within the checkup; the clinician determines appropriate tests.

What happens if reports are ready after I leave?

Agree how to obtain each report, whether translation is needed and who will provide the clinical explanation. A report handed over later is different from a further appointment, which needs separate confirmation.

Public and private options can be discussed. Institution requirements, appointment availability and medical charges must be confirmed for your request.

Before you request coordination

  • Share the service or appointment, approximate dates and communication language.
  • Tell us whether an appointment is already booked and whether you need accompaniment, written translation or travel support.
  • Mention whether relevant records exist; do not send passport numbers, payment details or full medical files in the initial inquiry.

Ask about your support and fee

The coordination fee is discussed for your request. Confirm the support included, time on site and any separately charged hospital or travel services.

Questions before you travel

How many days should I allow?

Some initial checkup visits may be planned around 1-2 days. Report availability, additional tests and clinician follow-up can take longer; confirm those dates separately.

Can I choose a package from a list?

Ask the provider for an itemized package and consultation requirements. IntersectionMed can support communication but does not select medically necessary tests.
