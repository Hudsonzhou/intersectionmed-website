Local support. Clear responsibilities.

IntersectionMed coordinates shorter healthcare visits for international visitors to Shenzhen. We help with the process around the appointment; licensed providers deliver the care.

A local point of contact for an unfamiliar process

IntersectionMed helps international patients navigate the non-clinical parts of a planned healthcare visit: communicating with hospitals, arranging appointments, understanding the visit process and coordinating language support.

We work around the service you are seeking, your travel dates and the communication support you need. Public and private hospital options can be discussed; availability and clinical suitability are confirmed by the institution.

For questions about who will assist you and the proposed support, contact intersectionmed@gmail.com. Replies are usually within 1-2 business days.

Built around a focused visit

What we focus on

Health checkups, dental checks and basic care, and digestive consultations or endoscopy appointments. TCM and rehabilitation consultations are optional requests.

A short visit starts with a feasible appointment schedule, not a promise to finish treatment. One or two appointments may be possible for some requests after provider confirmation.

What we clarify with you

The institution and campus, language needs, time on site, report handover and coordination fees. Public and private settings can be discussed without implying a standing partnership with a particular hospital.

Ask who will assist you and how changes will be handled before confirming support.

Who is responsible for each part of the visit?

Part of the visitRole
Coordination and communicationIntersectionMed helps with appointment communication, interpretation, navigation and travel logistics within the agreed scope.
Clinical assessment and careThe healthcare institution and licensed clinicians determine consultation, tests, treatment, preparation instructions and medical recommendations.
Travel and personal decisionsYou decide whether to proceed after reviewing provider information, support arrangements, fees and travel requirements.

Before agreeing to support

  • Confirm the proposed appointment or hospital setting and any requirements still outstanding.
  • Discuss the coordination fee, included support, additional costs and payment arrangements.
  • Ask who will assist you, which languages are available and how schedule changes are handled.
  • Share only general planning information initially; discuss an appropriate channel before sending sensitive records.

IntersectionMed is not a hospital or healthcare provider and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Medical decisions are made solely by licensed physicians and healthcare institutions.
