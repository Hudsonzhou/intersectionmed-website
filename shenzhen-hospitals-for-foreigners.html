Public and private hospitals: planning access in Shenzhen

Compare registration, language, location and billing before choosing an institution for a planned visit.

Compare three service settings

SettingQuestions to ask
Public hospital outpatient serviceWhich department and campus? How is a foreign passport registered? Is an appointment required?
Private hospital or clinicDoes it offer the service requested? How are clinician details, written charges and follow-up confirmed?
International departmentDoes it cover the appointment requested, which languages are offered and how are fees and insurance handled?

International departments may be part of a public or private institution; they are not a guarantee of every specialty or language.

Check the source of an appointment

Use official institution channels and the Shenzhen Government municipal hospital directory as starting points. The directory does not cover every private provider and is not a statement of partnership with IntersectionMed.

  • Confirm the institution's full name, campus and official contact channel.
  • Check that the department handles the type of consultation requested.
  • Ask which identity documents and records are required.
  • Ask how fees, receipts and follow-up contacts are provided.

Language and payment need separate confirmation

An English website does not establish that every department offers an English-speaking clinician. Check consultation interpretation, written reports and payment support separately.

If using insurance, ask the provider and insurer about direct billing, pre-authorization and the required documents. Do not assume a foreign insurance policy will be accepted.

How IntersectionMed can support the choice

We can help communicate appointment options in public and private settings based on your requested service, dates, language and location. Licensed clinicians and institutions make clinical decisions. Ask about the proposed support and coordination fee before proceeding.

IntersectionMed is not a hospital or healthcare provider and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Medical decisions are made solely by licensed physicians and healthcare institutions.
