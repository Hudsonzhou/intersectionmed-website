A visit-planning timeline: what to confirm at each stage

Use a stage-by-stage checklist to keep appointment confirmation, travel and reports aligned.

Stage 1: while dates are flexible

Choose the type of visit you want to discuss, your language and an approximate travel window. Compare the provider setting and ask what information is required before an appointment can be considered.

Stage 2: before fixed arrangements

ConfirmWhy it matters
Appointment and campusAn inquiry or proposed date is not a confirmed booking.
Scope and feesInterpretation, waiting time, documents and travel coordination may be different parts of the request.
Clinical preparationThe provider may need a consultation, preparation or documents before the planned appointment.
Reports and follow-upThese may have a different timeline from the day at the hospital.

Stage 3: before arriving

  • Keep the Chinese address, appointment details and provider contact available offline.
  • Check the official entry requirements and your arrival route.
  • Confirm the interpreter or accompaniment meeting details if arranged.
  • Keep any clinician-issued instructions accessible in a language you understand.

Stage 4: before leaving Shenzhen

Check which documents are ready, which will follow later and who will contact you. Ask the care team about any further appointments and travel-related questions specific to your care.
