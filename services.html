What about other outpatient requests?

Women's and men's health questions can be discussed within checkup planning. Eye, skin, fertility or other specialist requests need individual provider confirmation. IVF cycles, implants, surgery and ongoing rehabilitation are not one- or two-appointment packages.

An initial assessment is not a completed treatment course. Read the fertility consultation boundaries.

Ask About Support and Fees

Questions to confirm

  • What support and time are included?
  • Which hospital, translation, transport or accommodation charges are separate?
  • Who receives each payment, and in which currency?
  • What happens if an appointment changes or the visit is cancelled?

Public and private hospital options

We can help coordinate appointment options in public and private settings. Compare department availability, language support, location, registration and billing before choosing. See the hospital access guide for questions to ask.

Can I request interpretation if I already have an appointment?

Yes, you can inquire about interpretation or accompaniment for an appointment you have arranged. Share the institution, date and support required so availability and scope can be discussed.

Does a short visit mean treatment will be completed?

An initial consultation or appointment is different from a full course of care. Tests, reports, further visits and travel timing depend on the healthcare institution and clinician.

IntersectionMed is not a hospital or healthcare provider and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Medical decisions are made solely by licensed physicians and healthcare institutions.
