Healthcare setting

Compare the setting as well as the appointment.

Public hospitals, private providers and international departments have different registration, communication and billing arrangements. Availability must be checked with the institution.

SettingWhat to clarify
Public hospitalThe department and campus, passport registration, booking channel and whether language support is needed.
Private providerThe service offered, clinician credentials, language arrangements, written fees and follow-up process.
International departmentWhether the department offers your requested service, what the fee includes and which languages are available.

Use the Shenzhen Government municipal hospital directory as one official starting point. A listing is not an endorsement or a statement of partnership with IntersectionMed.
