Patient Guides / Costs and scope

Health checkup costs: what to confirm in a quote

Compare hospital charges, coordination support and travel expenses before making arrangements.

Separate the three parts of your budget

CostWhat to clarify
Healthcare providerConsultation, package items, any additional tests, reports and follow-up appointments.
Coordination and language supportAppointment support, interpretation time, accompaniment, document translation and post-visit communication.
Travel and daily expensesFlights or rail, accommodation, local transport and companion arrangements.

IntersectionMed coordination fees are discussed for your request. Contact us with the appointment type, dates and support you need; hospital and travel charges also require confirmation.

Compare scope before comparing totals

  • Does the hospital quote list the actual items and consultations included?
  • What could be charged separately, and who approves additional costs?
  • Are English documents, interpretation and certified translations separate?
  • Does support include waiting time, travel between sites or another visit?
  • Which payee, currency, payment timing and receipt apply to each charge?

Discuss changes before paying

Ask how changes, cancellations, deposits and third-party charges would be handled. Appointment availability, the final clinical plan and the cost of additional care can change; request clarification for the particular booking.

Request a discussion for your visit

A useful inquiry includes the service you are considering, approximate dates, whether a hospital is already chosen and any interpretation or accompaniment needs. Ask about support and the applicable coordination fee.

IntersectionMed is not a hospital or healthcare provider and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Medical decisions are made solely by licensed physicians and healthcare institutions.
