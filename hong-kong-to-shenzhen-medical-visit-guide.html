Patient Guides / Travel and arrival

Hong Kong to Shenzhen: coordinate the whole journey

Check entry, connections and the final hospital journey before fixing an appointment itinerary.

Check entry requirements for each part of the trip

Check the requirements for your passport and itinerary with Hong Kong Immigration and Mainland immigration authorities. Do not assume eligibility for one part of the journey confirms the other.

Check any return to Hong Kong as well as the first arrival. IntersectionMed coordinates travel logistics; entry decisions and requirements come from the authorities.

Compare routes using the final destination

Possible connectionConfirm before choosing
Cross-border railCurrent departure and arrival stations, ticket availability, document checks and the onward hospital journey.
Land border connectionCurrent crossing hours, transport on both sides and the final journey to your hotel or institution.
Airport and onward transferArrival time, the transfer operator's current route and the appointment arrival window.

These are planning categories, not live timetables. Confirm current routes and operating information with the operator.

Allow for more than scheduled travel time

Consider flight delays, immigration, luggage, transfers and registration before deciding whether arrival and a hospital appointment can share a day. Ask the institution whether a later arrival would affect the booking.

Coordinate the return separately

Check any provider instructions, documents still pending and further appointments. Confirm accommodation, return transport and entry requirements for the return route before making fixed plans.
