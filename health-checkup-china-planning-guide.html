Prepare for a Shenzhen health checkup

Separate package details, provider instructions, the day at the hospital and report follow-up.

Before choosing an appointment

Ask the provider what the package includes, which clinical consultation is available and whether any items require separate booking. The clinician determines what is medically appropriate; a larger list of tests is not itself a recommendation.

Receive preparation from the provider

Confirm how to receive and understand instructions about attendance, food or drink, medication questions and documents. Ask the care team about any unclear instruction; do not rely on a generic travel checklist as medical preparation.

Organize the day and the documents

  • Original identity document required by the institution.
  • Appointment location and registration time.
  • Relevant earlier reports requested by the provider, shared through an agreed channel.
  • Questions for the clinician and the interpretation scope if arranged.

Separate the checkup from the report timeline

An initial visit may fit around 1-2 days, while some reports or consultations may follow later. Ask when each report is expected, in which language, whether it can be accessed remotely and who provides the clinical explanation.
